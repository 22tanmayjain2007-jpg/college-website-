Learning Approach & Industry-Oriented Education

We believe computer science cannot be mastered through lectures alone. Our pedagogical framework embraces experiential learning, peer programming, live industry capstones, and active participation in global open-source ecosystems.

The 4-Year Engineering Progression Pathway:
  1. Foundation Year: Computational thinking, Data Structures, Discrete Mathematics, and Computer Architecture.
  2. Core Specialization: Object-Oriented Design, Operating Systems, Database Management, and Computer Networks.
  3. Applied Intelligence: Machine Learning, Cloud Native Systems, Full-Stack Architecture, and Information Security.
  4. Capstone & Industry Incubation: Multi-disciplinary research projects, industry internships, and enterprise deployments.
Core Computing Domains (Knowledge Taxonomy):
Artificial Intelligence & Neural Systems
Designing autonomous algorithms, natural language processors, and computer vision models capable of real-time cognition.
Distributed Systems & Cloud Infrastructure
Building scalable, fault-tolerant enterprise backends using microservices, containers, and serverless architectures.
Cybersecurity & Cryptographic Defense
Safeguarding digital perimeters through threat modeling, ethical penetration testing, and zero-trust protocol design.

Curriculum benchmarked in accordance with national engineering education frameworks and ACM/IEEE Computing Guidelines.

Academic Programs

Industry-Aligned Computing Curricula

Explore our accredited undergraduate and professional certificate programs designed to prepare students for tomorrow's technology landscape.

Flagship Undergraduate

B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering

⏱️ Duration: 4 Years (8 Semesters) 🎯 Intake: 180 Seats

A thorough study of fundamental computer science, software engineering principles, algorithm design, hardware-software integration, and scalable system architectures.

Major Subjects:

  • Data Structures & Algorithms
  • Operating Systems
  • Database Management
  • Computer Networks
  • Compiler Design
  • Software Engineering

Career Opportunities:

Software Development Engineer (SDE), Systems Architect, DevOps Engineer, Full Stack Developer, Technical Consultant.

Emerging Specialization

B.Tech Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning

⏱️ Duration: 4 Years (8 Semesters) 🎯 Intake: 60 Seats

Focused on machine perception, cognitive algorithms, generative deep neural networks, natural language processing, and ethical artificial intelligence deployment.

Major Subjects:

  • Machine Learning Algorithms
  • Deep Neural Networks
  • Natural Language Processing
  • Computer Vision
  • Reinforcement Learning
  • AI Ethics & Governance

Career Opportunities:

AI Engineer, Machine Learning Scientist, NLP Specialist, Computer Vision Engineer, Automation Researcher.

Big Data & Insights

B.Tech Data Science

⏱️ Duration: 4 Years (8 Semesters) 🎯 Intake: 60 Seats

Dedicated to computational statistics, massive distributed data processing, predictive modeling, data visualization, and decision intelligence systems.

Major Subjects:

  • Applied Statistical Methods
  • Big Data Processing (Hadoop/Spark)
  • Data Mining & Warehousing
  • Data Visualization & BI
  • Predictive Analytics
  • Cloud Data Engineering

Career Opportunities:

Data Scientist, Big Data Engineer, Business Intelligence Architect, Quantitative Analyst, Analytics Consultant.

Professional Certificate

Diploma in Full Stack Web Development

⏱️ Duration: 1 Year (2 Semesters) 🎯 Intake: 40 Seats

An intensive hands-on professional certificate covering modern frontend architectures, REST/GraphQL backend APIs, cloud deployment, and accessibility standards.

Major Subjects:

  • HTML5, CSS3 & Responsive Web
  • Modern JavaScript & TypeScript
  • Node.js & Express APIs
  • SQL & NoSQL Databases
  • Web Security & Auth (OAuth/JWT)
  • CI/CD & Cloud Hosting

Career Opportunities:

Full Stack Web Developer, Frontend Engineer, Backend API Developer, UI/UX Web Integrator, Web Application Specialist.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I arrange a physical laboratory tour?

Prospective candidates and parents can schedule a guided tour of our AI cluster and computing labs on any working Saturday by contacting the admissions desk.

Are research internships available for undergraduate students?

Yes. Students maintaining a CGPA of 8.0 and above may apply for faculty-supervised research fellowships starting from their 4th semester.

What programming languages are taught in the first year?

The foundation year curriculum emphasizes algorithmic thinking through C, C++, and Python alongside standard Git version control.
